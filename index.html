About CozyNest

Licensed home child care, done the right way.

CozyNest Home Child Care operates a licensed home-based daycare in Oshawa built to the same rigorous standards you'd expect from a recognized home child care agency like Wee Watch — comprehensive screening, ongoing home visits, low child-to-caregiver ratios, and a documented emergency and health & safety plan for every child.

We believe young children thrive with consistent caregivers, small groups, and a home environment that feels like family — paired with intentional, play-based early learning tied to Ontario's How Does Learning Happen? pedagogy.

Licensed & Monitored

Operated in compliance with Ontario's Child Care and Early Years Act, with regular inspections and documented policies.

Small Groups

Low ratios mean more one-on-one attention, safer supervision, and calmer days for little ones.

Real Family Home

A safe, child-proofed, welcoming home in Oshawa — not a big-box facility.

Licensing & Safety

Standards you can verify.

Modeled on the compliance framework used by recognized licensed home child care agencies such as Wee Watch, every CozyNest program follows a documented set of health, safety and licensing standards:

✔ CCEYA Licensed

Operating in full compliance with Ontario's Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014, including required documentation, ratios and reporting.

✔ Screening & Training

Vulnerable Sector Police Check, standard first aid & infant/child CPR certification, and ongoing professional development for every caregiver.

✔ Home Safety Inspections

Regular in-home health & safety visits covering fire safety, sleep environment, allergen management and child-proofing.

✔ Insurance Coverage

Commercial liability insurance in place to protect every family we serve.

✔ Emergency Preparedness

Written emergency management & evacuation plans, posted and reviewed with families and caregivers.

✔ Individual Care Plans

Documented allergy, medical and nutrition plans on file for every enrolled child.

FAQ

Good to know

Is CozyNest licensed by the province?

Yes. CozyNest operates as a licensed home child care provider under Ontario's Child Care and Early Years Act (CCEYA), following the same regulatory framework used by licensed home child care agencies such as Wee Watch.

What ages do you accept?

We welcome infants (6 weeks+) through school-age children up to 12 years, with age-appropriate programming for each group.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–6:00 PM, with flexible full-time and part-time schedules available.

How do I enroll?

Fill out the enrollment form below, and our team will reach out within 1–2 business days to schedule a home visit and tour.

What safety documentation can I review?

We're happy to share our licensing certificate, insurance certificate, most recent inspection report, and staff screening/training records during your enrollment visit.
